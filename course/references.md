## Provenance labels

Useful when reading or extending the course:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Workshop-original** | Steps written for almost-open; aligned with HTMAA competencies, not a class transcript. |
| **Scraped** | Themes or assignment bullets adapted from a saved public class page or deck text layer. |
| **Reconstructed** | Practice filled in where a source has a title and little prose. Do not quote as lecture transcript. |
| **Catalog-only** | A tool or machine named in catalogs; not required unless a week says so. |

## How this course is built

almost-open follows the **week spine and competency matrix** from the HTMAA course-design architecture (self-paced complete course: time slot, practice path, depth when evidence exists, assessment signal). It is an **open rewrite**, not a mirror of gated decks or a substitute for enrolled lab training.

Inspiration paths (local checkouts used when authoring):

- `~/src/how-to-make-anything/reports/course-design.md` — spine, matrix, journeys
- `~/src/how-to-make-anything/reports/study-guides/` — week practice themes
- `~/src/how-to-make-anything/reports/processes/` and `tools/` — coupons and catalog notes

## Tools policy

- Default worked paths use **FOSS** tools (FreeCAD, Inkscape, KiCad, PlatformIO, PrusaSlicer, Git, …).
- Commercial programs may appear as alternatives. They are not mandates.
- Measure on the machine you have. Shop training and posted settings win over guide numbers.
