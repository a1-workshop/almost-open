## What it does

A construction kit whose joint geometry follows spreadsheet parameters so a second material thickness does not require redrawing.

## Parameters

- `thickness` — caliper measurement
- `kerf` — from laser coupon
- `slot` — clearance-ladder winner (includes kerf)

## Practice

Bind FreeCAD sketches to those aliases; export DXF for the laser; assemble and document.
