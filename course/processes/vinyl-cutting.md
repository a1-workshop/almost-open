## What it does

A blade cuts adhesive vinyl (or similar film) along vector paths. You weed waste, then transfer the graphic.

## Design rules

- One-color art; convert text and strokes to paths.
- Weed border helps lift waste.
- Units in millimetres; match the cutter’s expected file type.

## Safety and shop

Complete local training. Record force, speed, and blade offset that cut the film without slicing the carrier.
