## What it does

A focused beam cuts or engraves sheet stock. Kerf depends on material, focus, power, speed, and passes.

## Coupons

- Kerf rectangle of known drawn width
- Clearance ladder around material thickness
- Sample joint types (finger, slot, snap/wedge)

## Safety

Shop training required. Never bypass interlocks. Publish settings next to photos of coupons.
